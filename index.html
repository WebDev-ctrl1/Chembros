Need to Know

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Do you deliver outside Port Elizabeth? +
Our primary delivery area is Port Elizabeth / Gqeberha. For orders outside PE, please contact us via WhatsApp at 067 739 1209 and we'll discuss options and courier arrangements.
Can I get bulk / wholesale pricing? +
Yes! We offer 5L and 25L volumes at significantly lower per-litre rates than our small bottles. Check the Bulk Volume tab in our products section, or fill out the bulk quote form above.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept EFT payments directly to our FNB account (Account Number: 63207348376). Cash on delivery may be available for local PE orders — confirm via WhatsApp when placing your order.
How does the bottle return policy work? +
For small volume purchases, bring back your empty bottle when you order again and save R3 off your next purchase. It's our way of encouraging reuse and reducing waste.
Are your products safe for home use? +
All ChemBros products are manufactured for standard household and commercial use. As with all cleaning products, follow the usage instructions and keep out of reach of children. Contact us if you need specific safety information for any product.
How quickly will my order be ready? +
Most orders are ready within 24–48 hours. Thick Bleach in 750ml is made to order — allow a little extra time. WhatsApp us to confirm availability and turnaround on specific products.
Can I resell ChemBros products? +
Absolutely. Many spaza shops and small retailers stock our products. Request a bulk reseller quote via our WhatsApp or the bulk quote form to discuss wholesale pricing.
How do I place an order? +
The easiest way is via WhatsApp — just tap any "Order via WhatsApp" button on the site and it'll open a pre-filled message. You can also call 067 739 1209 or use the contact form below.
